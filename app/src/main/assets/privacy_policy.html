Scope

Welcome to CashDash’s Privacy Statement. Your right to privacy and online security is central to our mission. This Privacy Statement describes CashDash’s collection, protection, disclosure, and use of the personal information provided to or collected through our service. This Privacy Statement applies to all of CashDash’s websites, mobile applications, and online services that link to or reference this Privacy Statement.

If you do not agree with any of the practices described in this Privacy Statement, please do not use our services.


Quick Overview

CashDash is a private financial ledger designed to give you absolute clarity over your spending. You choose what data to track and how you want to categorize it. Unlike public social ledgers, your expenses in CashDash are not made public. They are stored securely and visible only to you through your authenticated account.

In order to use CashDash and enable features like Cloud Sync, you will need to create an account, which requires providing registration information such as name, email address, and phone number. We use this information to manage your account and keep your data synchronized across devices.



Data We Collect

When you use CashDash, we may collect the following information about you:


Information You Provide to Us
  • Registration Information: When you sign up, you provide your name, email address, and phone number. We require an email address so that you can access your data from any device and we can contact you about your account.
  • User-Generated Content: When using the service, you provide information concerning expenses, balances, and categories. This includes transaction descriptions, amounts, custom category names, budget limits, and notes. This data is processed to generate your visual spending rings and analytics.
  • Feedback and Support: Any feedback, questions, or suggestions you send us are used to improve the service quality.

Automated Information Collection & Sensitive Permissions
  • Device and Usage Information: We receive information about the device you use, your operating system, and app version. We also collect data on how you interact with CashDash — such as the features you use, the frequency of your entries, and overall app performance diagnostics.
  • Location Information: We may receive approximate location data based on your IP address to ensure account security and regulatory compliance.
  • App Usage Stats (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): To power the "Smart Shopping Assistant" feature, CashDash requests access to your device's app usage statistics. This permission is used strictly locally on your device to detect when you open specific supported shopping or payment applications (e.g., UPI apps). We do not collect, store, or transmit your overall app usage history to our servers or any third parties.
  • System Alert Window (Overlays): CashDash requests the overlay permission to display the Smart Shopping Assistant widget over other apps. This is only triggered when interacting with relevant shopping apps to provide budgeting reminders, and it does not record your screen or read content from the underlying apps.


How We Use Your Data

We process your information to provide, support, and secure our service. Specific use cases include:

  • Financial Visualization: To calculate and display your wallet balance ring and category-wise spending analytics.
  • Account Synchronization: To ensure your financial history is available whenever you log in from a new device.
  • Security Monitoring: To detect and protect against unauthorized access, fraud, or other criminal activity. We monitor suspicious login attempts and device IDs to protect your account.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, perform product development, troubleshoot bugs, and improve our premium aesthetic design and functionality.
  • Communication: To alert you about new features, security issues, or critical account updates (e.g., password resets).


How We Share Your Information

Service Providers and Vendors

We use third-party service providers like Google Firebase to host our database (Firestore) and handle secure authentication. Legal Requirements

We will disclose personal information only when required by applicable law, such as to respond to subpoenas, court orders, or to protect the safety of our users or the integrity of our network.



Security & Data Ethics

We take the security of your financial data seriously. We use technical and administrative measures, including TLS encryption for data in transit and AES-256 encryption for data at rest within our cloud infrastructure.

Professional Ethics: We explicitly commit to not secretly tracking your location, not scanning your personal SMS/contacts, and not selling your financial behavior to data brokers.



Choice, Access & Data Retention

Your Choices
  • Personal Information: You can review and update your name, email, and phone number at any time from your Account Settings.
  • Notifications: You can opt-out of optional feature update emails while remaining subscribed to critical security notices.

Account Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete your account directly within the App by navigating to Profile → Delete Account.
  • Web/Email Deletion Request: If you no longer have the app installed, you can request account and data deletion by sending an email to info@cashdash.co.in from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  • Effect of Deletion: Completing this action (or upon processing your email request) triggers a permanent wipe of your authentication record, profile data, and entire transaction history from our servers within 7 days. This action is irreversible.

Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to serve you and as required by law. If you delete your account, we initiate a wipe of your records immediately, though some diagnostic logs may persist in anonymized form for a short period as part of standard server maintenance.
